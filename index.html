Step 4

How Our Smart Signal Works

1

1. Check

Sensors at the stop line check if cars are waiting on each road.

2

2. Switch Early

The moment the green road is empty (after a 2 s safety gap), it turns red and gives green to the waiting road — no wasted time or petrol.

3

3. Stay Safe

Always uses 3 s yellow + 2 s all-red before switching, and caps green at 90 s so no road is ever stuck waiting forever.
